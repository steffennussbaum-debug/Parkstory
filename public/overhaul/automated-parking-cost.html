What does automated parking cost?

The price per space is only half of the comparison. Automated parking also changes how much of your building earns money, so compare the result for the whole project.

Short answer

Most TilePark systems from 25 spaces cost around $30,000 per space, and the larger the system, the lower the cost per space. Your offer states exactly what is included. The free feasibility study gives the figure for your site.

What the price per space depends on

  • Number of spaces. Elements such as transfer cabins and controls are shared by more spaces in a larger system.
  • Vertical movement. Car elevators between levels (TilePark), stackers above the conveyor grid (TilePark Automated Stackers) or a central lift (TilePark Tower).
  • Number of transfer cabins. More cabins handle more cars at peak times.
  • Site geometry. Column grid, clear height and the shape of the plot decide how the grid is laid out.
  • EV charging. How many cars charge, and the electrical capacity available on site.
  • Service. The scope and term of the service contract.

What to compare between offers

Ask every supplier to state what the price includes, so that offers can be compared line by line:

  • Conveyors, lifts or stackers, and transfer cabins
  • Controls, software, the operator platform and the app
  • Installation and commissioning
  • EV charging hardware and load management
  • Service contract, response times and spare-parts availability

Building works such as the structure, fire protection, ventilation and the electrical supply belong in the comparison too, whoever provides them.

From parking area to value: three steps

  1. Recovered parking area. What TilePark frees compared with ramps and drive aisles: more than 150 sq ft per space on our calculator.
  2. Permitted building area. How much of it zoning lets you build and use. This can be all of it, part of it or none, depending on the site.
  3. Realizable value. What the market pays for that area, sold or rented, after fit-out costs.

The case study on our website follows these three steps for an example building that moves from a loss of $1.0 million to a profit of $1.3 million. Its calculator lets you enter your own figures.

Questions on this topic

Is TilePark more expensive than a conventional garage?

Per space, most TilePark systems cost about as much as a conventional garage. The difference lies in what you gain: without ramps and drive aisles, floor area becomes available that you can build, sell or rent, where zoning permits.

Does the price include the building?

The building structure is part of the building budget. Your offer states exactly what TilePark supplies and what the building provides.

Why is the cost per space lower in larger systems?

Shared elements such as transfer cabins, lifts and controls are spread over more spaces.
