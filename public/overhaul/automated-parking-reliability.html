What happens when a machine in an automated garage stops?

Reliability depends on which machines every car passes through. In TilePark the horizontal movement is distributed across many conveyors; the elements shared by many cars are planned and agreed per project.

Short answer

In the TilePark grid, every conveyor has its own controller and at least four drive motors, and cars are routed around a stopped conveyor. Only a car standing on that conveyor waits for the repair. Lifts, stackers, transfer cabins, the power supply and the control network are shared by the cars they serve, so their number, redundancy and the service response are planned and agreed for each project.

Measured, targeted, guaranteed

Three kinds of figures are easy to confuse, so we keep them apart:

  • Measured: results from systems in operation, with the measurement period, the sample and the definitions. We publish these once they are documented: to be published.
  • Design targets: what we build to, such as 99.5 % availability and the goal of solving 99 % of faults remotely.
  • Contractual guarantees: values agreed for your project and written into the contract, such as availability, retrieval time and service response time.

How the system is built to keep running

  • Every conveyor runs under load on our test rig before it ships, and every component carries a serial number.
  • Proven industrial components inside our own design, robust and widely available.
  • 24/7 service, remote diagnostics, a technician on site when needed, and spare parts for at least 30 years.

Questions on this topic

Can every car still be retrieved if a conveyor stops?

On the grid, yes: the other conveyors route cars around a stopped one. Only a car standing on the stopped conveyor itself waits for the repair.

What happens during a power failure?

The power concept, including how cars are retrieved during an outage, is part of the design we agree with you for your building.

Does TilePark have a single point of failure?

Horizontal movement does not depend on a single machine. Shared elements, such as lifts, transfer cabins, the power supply and, in TilePark Tower, the central lift, are planned with the redundancy and service response agreed for your project.

Who services the system?

Our own team: 24/7 service, remote diagnostics, a technician on site when needed, and spare parts for at least 30 years.
