How long does it take to get your car back?

Two figures matter: how long one driver waits, and how many cars the system delivers per hour at the busiest time of day.

Short answer

It depends on the size and layout of the system, the number of free positions, lifts or stackers and transfer cabins, and how many drivers want their car at the same time. We define the retrieval time and the peak-hour throughput with you for your project and write them into the contract. With the app, drivers can call their car ahead so it is waiting when they arrive.

What determines retrieval time

  • Distance on the grid. How far the car travels in X and Y to the cabin or lift.
  • Free positions. A few free positions let the grid move cars aside; how many a layout needs depends on its size, its shape and the required retrieval times.
  • Vertical movement. The number and speed of car elevators, stackers or the central lift.
  • Transfer cabins. More cabins serve more drivers at the same time.
  • Peak demand. How many drivers leave in the same hour, for example in the morning rush.
  • Calling ahead. A request from the app starts the retrieval before the driver arrives.

Retrieval time and throughput are different

Retrieval time is the time from a request until the car is ready in the transfer cabin. Throughput is the number of cars the system delivers per hour at peak. A system can be quick for one car and still be too slow in the rush hour, so both belong in the specification, as a typical and a maximum value.

How we specify them

In the feasibility study we lay out the grid, lifts or stackers and transfer cabins for your site and show how fast the system delivers cars. The agreed values go into the contract.

Questions on this topic

Is retrieval slower at peak times?

Peak demand is what the system is designed for: the number of lifts and transfer cabins is chosen so that the agreed peak-hour throughput is met.

Can drivers call their car in advance?

Yes. Drivers tell the app they are leaving, and the car is moved to the transfer cabin so it is waiting when they arrive.

What retrieval time can we put in the contract?

That depends on your layout and peak demand. We define typical and maximum retrieval times and the peak-hour throughput with you, and write them into the contract.
